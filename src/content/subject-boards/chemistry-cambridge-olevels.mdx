Cambridge O Level Chemistry (5070) is examined across three components — a multiple-choice paper, a structured theory paper carrying half the marks, and a practical assessment, sat either as the laboratory test or the written Alternative to Practical. For students heading towards A Level sciences, it builds exactly the habits they will need, and our teaching puts those first.

From atomic structure and bonding through the mole concept, acids and bases, and redox into organic foundations, the syllabus rewards students who can explain chemical behaviour precisely and handle quantitative problems with confidence. We focus on the step-by-step method and the correct chemical language examiners expect, so marks are not lost to vague or disordered answers.

Lessons are live and capped small, with the close attention that turns difficult topics into dependable ones.
