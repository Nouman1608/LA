## The time difference, in plain numbers

* Pakistan Standard Time (PKT) is UTC+5.
* Saudi Arabia (KSA) is UTC+3 — two hours behind Pakistan.
* UAE (Gulf Standard Time) is UTC+4 — one hour behind Pakistan.

So a class that runs at 7:00 PM in Lahore falls at 5:00 PM in Riyadh and Jeddah, and 6:00 PM in Dubai and Abu Dhabi — squarely inside the after-school, before-dinner window most Gulf households use for homework and tuition.
